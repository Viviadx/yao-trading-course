三、支撑区判断方法 How to Identify Support Zones

1

多次触底不破 Multiple Bounces

价格多次跌到某个区域又弹回来 = 支撑区。BTC 在 $62K-$64K 反复止跌（2/25, 2/28, 3/1）。
Price bounces off the same level multiple times = support zone.

2

放量反转区 High Volume Reversal Zone

在某个价格大量成交后反转 = 有大买单堆积。3/4 在 $66K-$67K 放量拉起 → 新支撑。
Heavy volume at a price level followed by reversal = big buyers stepped in.

3

前阻力变支撑 Previous Resistance Becomes Support

突破阻力后，原来的阻力变成支撑（角色互换）。3/4 突破 $70K → 回调到 $70K 不破就是新支撑。
Once broken, resistance flips to support (role reversal).

4

抬高的低点 Higher Lows in Uptrend

每次回调的低点越来越高 = 上升趋势中的动态支撑。2/28 低 $62,011 → 3/2 低 $63,864 → 低点在抬高。
Each pullback low is higher than the last = dynamic support in uptrend.

一、什么是支撑与阻力？ What are Support & Resistance?

核心定义 Core Definitions

为什么支撑和阻力有效？ Why Does S/R Work?

价格记忆 Price Memory：市场参与者会记住他们在某个价位买入或卖出的经历。
Market participants remember the prices where they bought or sold.

三种心理力量 Three Psychological Forces：

角色 Role在支撑位的行为 Behavior at Support
之前在这里买入的人 Prior buyers价格回来 → "我是对的，加仓" → 买入增加
Price returns → "I was right, add more" → more buying
之前在这里卖出的人 Prior sellers价格回来 → "后悔卖早了，这次不卖" → 卖压减少
Price returns → "I sold too early, won't sell again" → less selling
观望的人 Sideliners价格回来 → "上次没买，这次买" → 新买入
Price returns → "Missed it last time, buying now" → new buying
三股力量合一 = 支撑生效 | All three forces combine = support holds

二、如何在图表上识别支撑与阻力 How to Identify S/R on Charts

1

水平线法 Horizontal Line Method

找到价格多次反弹或受阻的水平区域，画一条水平线。至少2次触及才有效，3次以上更可靠。
Find horizontal zones where price bounced or stalled multiple times. Draw a horizontal line. At least 2 touches to be valid, 3+ for reliability.

操作：在TradingView上用"水平线"工具，连接多个反弹低点（支撑）或回落高点（阻力）。
Action: Use the "Horizontal Line" tool on TradingView, connecting bounce lows (support) or rejection highs (resistance).

2

整数关口法 Round Number / Psychological Level

$50,000、$60,000、$70,000、$100,000 等整数位自带心理效应。大量挂单堆积在整数附近。
Round numbers like $50K, $60K, $70K, $100K carry psychological weight. Limit orders cluster around these levels.

⚠️ 整数关口不是精确的线，通常上下浮动 1-2%。例如 "$70K 阻力" 实际可能在 $69,000-$71,500。
Round numbers are not exact — they typically fluctuate ±1-2%. "$70K resistance" might actually be $69,000-$71,500.

3

成交量密集区法 Volume Profile / High Volume Node

成交量最大的价格区域 = 最多人在这里交易 = 强支撑/阻力。可以用 TradingView 的 Volume Profile 指标查看。
The price zone with the highest volume = most participants traded here = strong S/R. Use TradingView's Volume Profile indicator.

4

K线形态确认法 Candlestick Confirmation

在潜在支撑位出现锤子线 (Hammer)、看涨吞没 (Bullish Engulfing) = 支撑被确认。
在潜在阻力位出现射击之星 (Shooting Star)、看跌吞没 (Bearish Engulfing) = 阻力被确认。
Hammer / Bullish Engulfing at potential support = support confirmed.
Shooting Star / Bearish Engulfing at potential resistance = resistance confirmed.

💡 这就是 Day 1 和 Day 2 的连接点 — K线形态 + 支撑阻力 = 更高胜率的判断。
This connects Day 1 and Day 2 — candlestick patterns + S/R = higher probability analysis.

5

角色互换法 Polarity Flip / Role Reversal

最重要的概念之一：当价格突破阻力后回踩该区域，如果不跌破，原来的阻力就变成了新支撑（反之亦然）。
One of the most important concepts: When price breaks above resistance then pulls back to that zone, if it holds, the old resistance becomes new support (and vice versa).

BTC 实例：3/4 突破 $70K 阻力区后，$70K 附近变成新的支撑区。如果回调到 $70K 止跌 = 角色互换成功。
BTC example: After breaking $70K resistance on 3/4, the $70K area becomes new support. If pullback stops at $70K = successful polarity flip.
